Two rules, that's it: start a message with "Dori" and it steps in on request — everything below is that. Links and files (a YouTube URL, a dragged-in PDF or photo) just work on their own, no "Dori" needed.

Copy, paste, go

Six lines to try in your agent, in order. Say them as they are, or fill in the brackets first — either way it knows what to do with what's missing.

1 · Start a project

Dori, start a new project called [Name]

No files needed yet — it scaffolds an empty project folder. Attach files any time after; the same command reads what's there and files it in.

2 · Save a video

Dori, store this YouTube link: https://youtu.be/hiYTq9xnkPw?si=kOcnI3mlmqueWaTj

Swap in any YouTube URL. You'll have a searchable transcript in your vault in under a minute, plus a small local page you can click open and browse.

3 · Create your profile

Dori, create my profile: I'm [Name], [Role] at [Company]

Or drop in a CV or your LinkedIn profile as a PDF first, and ask it to fill this in from that instead of typing it out. On your LinkedIn profile, open the Resources button (below your name) and pick Save to PDF — it opens in a new tab, ready to drag in.

Also worth adding

Dori, my LinkedIn is [profile URL]

Saves it alongside your profile, so it's there the next time anything needs it.

4 · Create a brand

Dori, create a brand called [Name] — here's our site: [URL]

It reads the site itself and writes up positioning, voice, and colors from what's actually there. Reuse it later with "write this in the [Name] voice."

5 · Look someone up

Dori, who's [Name] from [Company]?

Works today for free, using your agent's own web search. Add a free Tavily key later (1,000 searches/month, no card required) for a version that links what it finds straight into your vault instead of just answering in chat.

6 · Add WhatsApp

Dori, add WhatsApp as a channel

Runs the pairing script and shows a QR code to scan, right there. Needs a spare WhatsApp number, never your main one. Why it's safe →

Get more out of Dori Mini

Once you've actually captured a few things, these get useful.

Minutes from a transcript

Dori, turn this into minutes

Paste a meeting transcript along with it — comes back as Attendees · Topics · Decisions Log · Action Items, each tagged with who owns it.

Check your inbox

Dori, what do I need to resolve?

Lists anything it couldn't confidently file on its own — usually because a capture could belong to more than one project. A safeguard, not a bug.

Recall a decision

Dori, what did I decide about [topic]?

Comes back empty the very first time — that's expected. Capture a couple of things first, then ask again.

Check your tasks

Dori, what are my tasks?

Reads straight from your task store — open items, sorted by due date. Read-only for now: it lists what's there, it doesn't add new ones.

Optional, skip freely
Fathom lets it pull meeting recordings straight from your account instead of you pasting a transcript — set it up any time from the full guide. Nothing above needs it.

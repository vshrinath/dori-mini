No commands to learn. One rule, one first thing, then a list to steal from.

Not set up yet? One line, once.

macOS/Linuxcurl -fsSL https://mini.mydori.app/install.sh | sh
Windowsirm https://mini.mydori.app/install.ps1 | iex

The rule: start the message with Dori. That's the entire trigger — every line on this page is one sentence beginning that way.

The exception: links and files need no trigger at all. Paste a YouTube URL, drop in a PDF or a photo of a receipt, and it files itself.

Do one thing first.

Say this in whichever agent you wired up. Any YouTube link works — this one is a 46-minute podcast episode.

Dori, store this YouTube link: https://youtu.be/hiYTq9xnkPw
~/dori-vault/yt/2026-08-24-blind-spots-to-big-bets.md
# Blind Spots to Big Bets Source · youtube.com/watch?v=hiYTq9xnkPw · 46:08 ## Summary ## Where founders misread the market ## The bet that changed the model ## Full transcript

About a minute later, that file is on your disk. The headings are the video's own chapters, used as they are rather than invented. From that moment it's searchable — Dori, what did that video say about pricing?

Everything else you can say.

Same shape as the line above. Fill in the brackets, or leave them — it'll ask.

Capture something

Dori, start a new project called [Name]

Scaffolds an empty folder. Attach files any time after.

Dori, create my profile: I'm [Name], [Role] at [Company]

Or drop in a CV and let it read that instead of typing it out.

Dori, create a brand called [Name] — here's our site: [URL]

Reads the site and writes up positioning, voice and colours.

Dori, turn this into minutes

Paste a transcript with it. Comes back as Attendees · Topics · Decisions · Action Items, each with an owner.

Dori, add WhatsApp as a channel

Shows a QR code to scan. Needs a spare number, never your main one. Why it's safe →

Ask it something

Dori, what did I decide about [topic]?

Empty the very first time — that's expected. Capture a few things, then ask again.

Dori, who's [Name] from [Company]?

Uses your agent's own web search. Free, works today.

Dori, what are my tasks?

Open items from the real task store, sorted by due date.

Dori, add a task: [what] by [when]

Same store — also fills itself from a meeting's Action Items once minutes exist.

Dori, what do I need to resolve?

Anything it couldn't confidently file, usually because it fits two projects. A safeguard, not a bug.

Dori, anything new in my inbox folder?

What landed in the watched folder since you last looked. Needs the optional install step.

Three things worth knowing.

None required. Each one makes a line above work better.

Your LinkedIn as a PDF

On your profile, open Resources under your name and pick Save to PDF. Drag that in and it fills your profile out for you.

A free Tavily key

1,000 searches a month, no card. Turns "who's [Name]" into something that files what it finds, rather than just answering in chat.

Fathom, if you use it

Pulls meeting recordings straight from your account instead of you pasting a transcript. Add the key by rerunning the installer.
